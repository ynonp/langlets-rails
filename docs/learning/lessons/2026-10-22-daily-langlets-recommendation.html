Quest: The Journey of a Daily Langlet 🎬

Why it matters. After a learner finishes the five starter quests, Langlets picks one fresh video a day in their language, based on their saved words and finished lessons. The video is imported into a full course before the learner is told about it, so tapping the card opens something already playable.

Who triggers it. A scheduled sweep (SendDailyChallengesJob) and, as a backup, the learner opening /daily_challenge. Heads up: in production, delivery is currently paused, so the card is prepared but no notification is sent.
