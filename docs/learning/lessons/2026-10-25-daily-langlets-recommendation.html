🎬 Quest: The Daily Langlets Delivery

Lesson of 2026-10-25 · studied at commit 4d6b564 · ~12 min

🎯 Quest briefing

The flow: every day after a learner's first five curated quests, Langlets picks one personalized video for them, imports it into a ready-to-study course, and only then sends a reminder.

Why it matters: it is the daily habit loop of the product. The "prepare before notify" ordering means a push never points at an empty or still-importing card.

Triggered by: SendDailyChallengesJob, a Solid Queue recurring job that runs every 5 minutes in production (config/recurring.yml:2-4). Opening /daily_challenge can also ensure today's row exists (per docs/architecture.md).

⚠️ Current state: production has config.x.daily_langlets_delivery_enabled = false (config/environments/production.rb:6), so the scheduler job returns immediately there.
