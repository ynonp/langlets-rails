Quest: The Journey of a Daily Pick 🎬

Daily Langlets: from a 5-minute cron tick to a ready-made course

🎯 Quest intro

The flow: after a learner finishes the 5 starter quests, each new local day Langlets picks one YouTube video for them (using Gemini search on the pipeline host), pre-builds a full course from it, and only then shows the card and notifies them.

Why it matters: it is the habit loop of the product: a personal video that matches the learner's saved words and lessons, ready to study with no waiting. The card is only exposed once the course and translation are really ready (DailyChallenge#unlocked?).

Triggers: the send_daily_challenges recurring job (every 5 minutes) or the learner opening /daily_challenge within 30 minutes of their reminder time.
