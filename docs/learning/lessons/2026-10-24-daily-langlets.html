Quest: The Daily Langlet 📬

From a 5-minute cron tick to a push notification on a learner's phone.

🎯 Quest intro

Flow: Daily Langlets — the "daily video we hope you'll like". Once a learner finishes the five starter quests, each new local day gets a DailyChallenge row (day 6, 7, …) holding a personalized video that Gemini chose.

Why it matters: it's the daily habit loop: a fresh, level-appropriate video, already imported as a course, waiting for the learner at their chosen reminder time.

Triggered by: SendDailyChallengesJob, scheduled "every 5 minutes" in production (config/recurring.yml:2-4). Opening /daily_challenge can also kick off preparation.

⚠️ Production delivery is currently paused (commit 5b8c96a, 2026-10-17). You'll learn exactly how.
