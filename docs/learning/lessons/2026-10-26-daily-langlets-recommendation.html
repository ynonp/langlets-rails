🎯 Quest: The Daily Langlet Pick 🎬

Quest intro

Flow: "Daily Langlets" personalized video recommendation (day 6 onwards of a learner's practice plan).

Why it matters: After the five curated starter quests, each day a learner gets one video they should enjoy and learn from, already imported and ready to play. It keeps them returning, so it is a core retention loop. It also has to avoid wasted AI calls and duplicate imports.

Triggered by: a Solid Queue recurring job every 5 minutes (config/recurring.yml) or by the learner opening /daily_challenge.

⚠️ As of 2026-10-17 production delivery is paused (config.x.daily_langlets_delivery_enabled = false), but the page can still prepare a card.
