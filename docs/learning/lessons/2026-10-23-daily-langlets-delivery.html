Quest: The Journey of a Daily Langlet 📬

🎯 Quest briefing

Flow: the personalized Daily Langlets pipeline — from a scheduler tick to an email/push saying "here's a video we hope you'll like".

Why it matters: after five curated starter quests, every learner gets one AI-picked video per day in their learning language, already imported and ready, so the habit loop never asks them to wait. Getting it wrong means duplicate, stale or empty notifications to real users.

Triggered by: Solid Queue's recurring schedule (send_daily_challenges, every 5 minutes, config/recurring.yml). ⚠️ Production currently has it paused (config/environments/production.rb:6).
